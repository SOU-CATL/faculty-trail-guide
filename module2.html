An Alternative Path — Start Here Now

While You Wait: Check Your Course Against Federal Requirements

The trail ahead isn't closed — it's under construction. And the best preparation you can do before Brickfield launches is to understand exactly what your online course needs to meet federal standards.

📋 New federal requirement: Under the 2024 update to Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), all public universities must bring web content and digital course materials into compliance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards by April 2027. This is a legal requirement — and Brickfield will be your tool for meeting it.

CATL's Distance Education Federal Requirements Checklist covers the full picture of federal compliance for online courses — not just digital accessibility, but all five areas every DE course is required to meet:

  • Course Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs)
  • Regular Substantive Interaction (RSI)
  • Verification of Student Identity
  • FERPA
  • Digital Accessibility — WCAG 2.1 Level AA (the ADA Title II requirement)

Working through this checklist now is the most valuable thing you can do while Brickfield is being installed. You'll know exactly what your course needs to fix — and when the toolkit arrives, you'll be ready to use it.
