The Trail Metaphor

A Picture at the Trailhead

The best trail maps aren't walls of text — they're sketches. A quick visual that tells you at a glance: here's the elevation, here are the landmarks, here's what to watch for. Your course content can do the same. A well-designed infographic gives students a visual anchor — something to return to when the reading feels long or the week feels overwhelming.

The UDL Connection

Dual Coding and Working Memory

Dual coding theory tells us that information is retained more effectively when it's presented in both verbal and visual forms. For students with ADHD, dyslexia, or processing differences, a visual summary is not decoration — it's an access ramp.

A Gemini-generated infographic in Moodle:

  • Reduces working memory load by externalizing key relationships visually
  • Gives students a quick re-entry point when they lose their place
  • Supports students who process visually before they can process text
  • Signals that multiple ways of knowing are valued in your course

Who benefits most: Students with ADHD, dyslexia, visual-spatial learners, and students for whom English is not a first language.

Use the Gemini Gem to generate an infographic for one of your Moodle courses, then insert it so students see it immediately.
